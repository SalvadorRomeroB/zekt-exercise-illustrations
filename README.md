# Zekt exercise illustrations

Two-frame line-art illustrations (start and end position) of strength exercises,
as used in the Zekt training app. They are adapted from the Everkinetic exercise
illustrations and shared under **CC BY-SA 4.0**.

- `svg/everkinetic-<id>-start.svg` and `svg/everkinetic-<id>-end.svg`: 108 drawings, 216 files.
- `index.json`: for each drawing, its source, its files, and the exercises Zekt uses it for.
- `ATTRIBUTION.md`: credit and the list of changes.
- `LICENSE.md`: the full license text.

## Credit line
> Illustrations adapted from Everkinetic (github.com/everkinetic/data) by Zekt,
> CC BY-SA 4.0 (creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0).

## Why this repository exists
The app is distributed through app stores. Under CC BY-SA 4.0, anyone who
receives these adapted illustrations must be able to use them under that
license. This repository makes them available without the technical
restrictions an app store applies to the app binary.
