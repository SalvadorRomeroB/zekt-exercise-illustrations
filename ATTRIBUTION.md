## Changes made by Zekt
- Removed the white background layer.
- Added a body/equipment silhouette fill and a soft highlight over the target muscle.
- Split every exercise into separate start and end frames (`-start.svg`, `-end.svg`) and aligned them.
- Vector-traced three drawings from their source PNGs because no source SVG exists:
  `everkinetic-0003`, `everkinetic-0012`, `everkinetic-0047`.
- Recolored. In the Zekt app the colors follow the app theme; the files here use neutral default colors.
